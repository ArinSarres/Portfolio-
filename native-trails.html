Native Trails

For native trails I designed a system that makes trails more than just a walk. Find local trails, view their descriptions, and earn achievements while doing it!

Curious Kid

8 yrs old

  • Short attention span
  • Does not enjoy just “walks”
  • Would rather be gaming
Their journey
  1. Gets handed phone
  2. Follows GPS map
  3. Completes achievements
  4. Views their high score
  5. Shares with friends

Fitness Runner

26 yrs old

  • Likes accurate trail data
  • Likes knowing features like loops and elevation
  • Wants to be able to track progress
Their journey
  1. Find trail
  2. View trail descriptions
  3. Connect smartwatch
  4. Track stats
  5. Unlock achievements
  6. Share with friends
