Playbook Financial

Created a brand for a financial service tailored to professional athletes and their families, built to help them protect and grow their earnings during their careers and long after.

Audience
Professional athletes and the families who depend on their earnings
Deliverables
Name, logo, color, type, photography, pattern, website and merchandise
Tools
Figma and FigJam

The challenge

A pro career is short, and most of the money arrives in a few years. Athletes juggle signing bonuses, endorsement deals and taxes in every city they play in, often with a lot of people asking for a piece. Financial brands tend to talk to them with flash or with fear. Playbook needed to feel like the opposite: a calm, disciplined teammate who sticks around after the last game.

From Sideline to Playbook

SIDELINE FINANCIAL PLAYBOOK FINANCIAL

The first name, Sideline Financial, put the brand on the bench, watching. A playbook is the written plan a whole team trusts and runs together, which is exactly the service: a clear plan for income, taxes, investing and life after the game. The tagline from the first round stayed: the teammate that stays.

The mark

PLAYBOOKFINANCIAL

Every play a coach draws is made of X’s and O’s. The mark puts the two together: the athlete as a bold slab serif X, held inside an O that stands for the team around them. The heavy ring keeps it solid at every size, from an app icon to the back of a hoodie.

Five values shape every decision, from the photography to the website copy. They are set as a line of X’s and O’s, the same way they run across the top of the site.

Photography

Black and white, high contrast and focused on the work athletes put in, not the highlight reel. The mark sits in the frame like a watermark, and Playbook Blue is saved for a duotone when a moment needs to stand out.

The chalk play pattern

The supporting pattern is drawn like a whiteboard in the film room: X’s, O’s and routes, loose and hand made. It fills dark sections of the site at low opacity and turns up on merchandise at full strength.

Website

The homepage is built like a game plan. It opens on the promise, lays out the four phases of a career, then explains how the work runs: scout, draw up the play, run it together. Below is the full page, rebuilt in code with the brand fonts and colors. Scroll inside the window to read it.
